# MapOS code of conduct

MapOS is built in public for people with different backgrounds, abilities and levels of mapping
experience. Everyone participating in the project is expected to make that collaboration safe and
useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific and patient. Critique the work, never the person.
- Make space for different languages, accessibility needs and levels of technical experience.
- Treat location, identity and provider data as sensitive; do not post another person's private
  information in an issue, log or fixture.
- Disclose conflicts of interest, commercial relationships and licensing constraints that could
  affect a proposal.
- Stop behaviour when a maintainer says that it is unwelcome or unsafe.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing and repeated disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a security or privacy incident, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other
conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through a private repository channel when one is
available; do not expose the affected person in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close participation, or temporarily or permanently restrict access. They will minimise
disclosure and avoid conflicts of interest when handling a report.

This policy applies in repositories, reviews, community spaces and public representation of the
project.
